BATTLEMAGE — UNIT ROSTER
Sorted by Race and Tier
========================================

KEYWORDS
--------
  Taunt        — Must be attacked first
  Divine Shield — Absorbs the first hit taken
  Poisonous    — Kills any unit it deals damage to
  Reborn       — Comes back at 1 HP after dying once

========================================
HUMANS
========================================
Humans are a weapon-based faction. Their units synergize with equipping,
upgrading, and firing guns. Many effects trigger on attack or apply to a
specific weapon type.

--- Tier 1 ---

  Grunt           1/6
  Surge: the next weapon purchase or upgrade costs 2 less gold.

  Recruit         1/4
  Gains +1 ATK permanently each time a friendly unit dies.

  Gunslinger      1/4
  Death Knell: fires its weapon one final time when it dies.

--- Tier 2 ---

  Sharpshooter    3/4
  Deals bonus damage when attacking with a Sniper.

  Arms Dealer     1/5
  Reduces the cost of weapon upgrades.

  Brawler         3/5
  Fires back at enemies that attack it (counter-attack).

  Demolitionist   3/4
  Deals bonus damage when attacking with a Shotgun.

  Gunner          3/5
  Deals bonus damage when attacking with a Machine Gun.

--- Tier 3 ---

  Commander       1/6
  Aura: grants a bonus to all friendly Humans while alive.

  Quartermaster   3/5
  Surge: give a random friendly Human +1 weapon level.

  Breacher        3/5  [Default weapon: Shotgun]
  Surge: give this unit's weapon +2 levels.

  Ranger          3/4  [Default weapon: Sniper]
  Surge: give this unit's weapon +2 levels.

  Heavy           3/6  [Default weapon: Machine Gun]
  Surge: give this unit's weapon +2 levels.

--- Tier 4 ---

  Warlord         4/6
  Aura: all friendly weapons fire twice per attack.

  Duelist         3/6  [Default weapon: Pistol]
  Dual Wield: fires both its equipped weapon and its backpack weapon on
  each attack.

========================================
GOBLINS
========================================
Goblins are a swarm faction built around Goblin Peons — cheap 1/1 tokens
whose stats grow from a shared "stat budget." Many abilities permanently
increase that budget or manipulate Peons in combat.

--- Tier 1 ---

  Mob Runt        1/2
  On attack: summon a Goblin Peon.

  Grub Grabber    1/3
  Death Knell: permanently increase the Peon stat budget by 1.

  Sneak           1/1  [Poisonous]
  On kill: permanently increase the Peon stat budget by 1.

--- Tier 2 ---

  Pack Leader     2/3
  Aura: all other Goblins and Peons gain +2 ATK.

  Scrap Bomber    3/1
  Death Knell: deal 2 damage to a random enemy and summon a Peon.

  Bomb King       4/4
  Whenever a friendly Peon dies, deal 2 damage to a random enemy.

  Rabble Riser    2/4
  Surge: permanently increase the Peon stat budget by 2.

  Reckoner        3/4
  Avenge 2: permanently increase the Peon stat budget by 1.
  Death Knell: summon a Goblin Peon.

--- Tier 3 ---

  Brood Witch     1/5
  On attack: give all friendly Peons +3/+3 for this combat.

  Litter Lord     6/6
  Death Knell: summon a Goblin Peon with Reborn.

  Taskmaster      2/7
  Aura: all Peons spawned while this is alive use 2x the stat budget.

  Rage Brand      3/5
  Start of Combat aura: all Peons are given ATK equal to at least
  125% of their HP. Peons spawned mid-combat inherit this skew.

  Witch Doctor    2/6
  Death Knell: give a random friendly unit Reborn and Taunt.

--- Tier 4 ---

  Warchief        3/6
  Aura: whenever any friendly Goblin dies, summon a replacement Peon.

  Chaos Caller    5/5
  Death Knell: summon 4 Goblin Peons, each at 2x the stat budget.

========================================
MAGES
========================================
Mages are a spell faction. They use a Mage Shop to attach triggers
(On Attack, Death Knell, Start of Combat, When Attacked) to spells.
Spell Power is a board-wide stat that scales all spell effects.

--- Tier 1 ---

  Novice          1/3
  Aura: grants +1 Spell Power while alive.

--- Tier 2 ---

  Spellbinder     2/4
  Surge: equip the last spell that was bought. Triggers must be
  purchased separately in the Mage Shop.

  Runecaller      2/3
  Surge: discover a spell to equip, then immediately cast it.

  Amplifier       2/4
  Aura: all On Attack spell triggers fire one extra time per attack.

  Spellwright     2/3
  Surge: give a random allied Mage +1 level on a random trigger.

  Oracle          1/4
  Surge: give a random allied Mage a random keyword.

--- Tier 3 ---

  Arcane Conduit  2/4
  Surge: permanently increase Spell Power by 1.

  Wellspring      1/5
  Surge: deal damage equal to your current Spell Power to all enemies.

  Hexweaver       2/4
  Aura: all spell trigger fire counts are doubled during combat.

  Echo Caster     2/5
  Passive: all Start of Combat spells fire one additional time.

--- Tier 4 ---

  Arcanist        2/4
  Passive: whenever any spell fires, give your entire board +1/+1.

  Channeler       2/4
  Combat aura: all spell triggers fire one extra time while this
  unit is alive.

========================================
NEUTRAL
========================================
Neutral units have no race synergy but fit any board. Those without
explicit tier are baseline units with simple stats and keywords.

--- No Race / Untiered ---

  Footman         2/2
  No effect. Basic melee unit.

  Warrior         3/2
  No effect. High-attack melee unit.

  Viper           1/2  [Poisonous]
  No effect. Kills any unit it damages.

  Defender        1/4  [Taunt]
  No effect. Forces enemies to attack it first.

  Shielded Bot    2/2  [Divine Shield]
  No effect. Absorbs the first hit it takes.

  Harvest Golem   2/3  [Reborn]
  No effect. Comes back at 1 HP after dying once.

--- Tier 3 ---

  Grave Conductor  3/4
  Each friendly unit's Death Knell fires one additional time.

  Surge Master     3/5
  All friendly Surges fire twice.

========================================
SUMMONED TOKENS (not purchasable)
========================================

  Goblin Peon          1/1
  Base summon token. Stats scale with the shared Peon stat budget.

  Goblin Peon (Chain)  1/1
  Death Knell: summon a regular Goblin Peon (no chain recursion).
